Calendar

Days that change a week

Dates for festivals move. Public holidays do not. Check the year you are travelling.

15 January · National

John Chilembwe Day

A public holiday remembering Pastor John Chilembwe and the 1915 uprising.

Banks and many offices close. It is a day of speeches and church, not a festival laid on for visitors.

3 March · National

Martyrs’ Day

A national day of remembrance.

Expect closures. Plan fuel and meals the day before if you will be on the road.

6 July · National

Independence Day

The country marks independence from 1964 with official ceremonies and a long weekend feeling.

Cities are busier around the official events. Parks do not pause, but staff may be thinner.

Usually July · Southern Region

Mount Mulanje Porters’ Race

A fierce mountain race that grew out of the porters’ own route up and down the massif.

The date moves. If you want to watch or to keep off the main path that day, check with guides at Likhubula that month.

Usually late September · Southern Region

Lake of Stars

Malawi’s best-known music festival, held on the lake when the year’s edition goes ahead.

It is not guaranteed every year in the same bay. Buy tickets from the festival’s own channels and book a bed early. Do not trust a forwarded flyer.

Usually November · Central Region

Tumaini Festival

An arts festival at Dzaleka refugee camp, near Dowa, led by people who live there.

Go as a guest of the festival, not as a drop-in spectacle. Follow the organisers’ rules on photography.
