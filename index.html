The three regions

Northern Region

High plateaus, an escarpment mission town, and the deep-water bays of the northern lake. Likoma Island belongs to this region even though it sits far out in the lake.

Central Region

Lilongwe, the capital, plus the Salima lakeshore, Nkhotakota's restored woodland, and the rock-art hills of Dedza.

Southern Region

The great massif of Mulanje, the Zomba Plateau, Liwonde's elephants, Blantyre, and the Mangochi lakeshore including Cape Maclear.
