Editor, privacy, and the law

This page is the conspicuous notice for the public edition. It is not a government certificate.

Who edits this

This is a non-professional guide, not a registered company and not the Department of Tourism. The public editor name on this edition is the GitHub account bls21-kkhomba-oss.

A full personal name, a domicile in Malawi, a telephone number, and an email are not printed yet. Add those before you take payment, sell listings, or present the site as a business. Section 31 of the Electronic Transactions and Cyber Security Act, 2016 expects a professional online content provider to show them.

The same section lets a non-professional editor publish their name together with the name and address of the host, after the host has their identity details. Those details sit on the GitHub account that publishes this site. They are not repeated here.

Who hosts it

The host is GitHub, Inc., through GitHub Pages. GitHub’s published office, from its own terms, is 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, United States. GitHub is the intermediary. It is not the editor of the words.

What this site is allowed to be

Section 24 of that Act starts from freedom of online communication. The limits that matter here are child sexual content, incitement to racial hatred, xenophobia or violence, and other written law. This guide does not do those things. It also avoids treating living culture as a costume show. If a person or a business is named and wants a correction, open an issue on the GitHub repository. That is the right-of-reply path for this edition. There is no comment box, because a comment box would collect personal data.

Personal data

Saving a place, or a suggestion, writes it only into this browser. It is not uploaded. There is no account, no newsletter, and no analytics tool.

Opening the site does send your connection, including an IP address, to GitHub in the United States, because that is where the pages are served from. GitHub processes that as the host. This edition does not add a second transfer. Malawi’s Data Protection Act, 2024 is in force and is overseen by MACRA. A guide that does not hold a database of people is not, on these pages, a “significant” controller. If you later collect emails, booking details, or staff records, publish a proper privacy notice and check whether you must register.

Money

Nothing on this site is for sale. Prices mentioned for parks and lodges are reminders to confirm the real figure. They are not an offer. When you do advertise or take payment, the electronic-commerce part of the 2016 Act requires supplier details, honest descriptions, labelled adverts, and no unsolicited messages.

Photographs

Pictures are from Wikimedia Commons and keep the credit and licence the photographer chose. A lake photograph on a lakeshore page is a lake photograph, not proof of that exact lodge.
